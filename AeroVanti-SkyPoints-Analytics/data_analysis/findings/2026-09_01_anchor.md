# 01 — Anchor: the Flash Redemption redemption-rate numbers

**Decision it informs:** none directly — establishes the dual-track habit on a known number.
**Audience:** the analyst. **Window:** experiment = 2026-03-02 → 2026-05-24 (12 weeks).

## Question
Reproduce the pooled and per-tier redemption rate (share of assigned subjects with ≥ 1
redemption in the 12-week window) for control vs treatment, and confirm pandas = DuckDB.

## Grain
`fact_experiment_outcome` — one row per assigned subject (15,000; 7,500 per arm).

## Method (both tracks)
`AVG(CAST(redeemed_in_window AS INT))` by `arm_key`, and by `stratum × arm_key`.
`python/01_anchor.py` computes it in pandas and DuckDB; `utils.parity` asserts equality
(exact for these rates). `sql/01_anchor.sql` is the standalone SQL.

## Evidence
| | control | treatment | lift (pp) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pooled** (n = 7,500/arm) | 12.76% | 15.45% | **+2.69** |
| Blue (n = 3,500/arm) | 6.11% | 9.89% | +3.77 |
| Silver (n = 1,900/arm) | 10.84% | 13.00% | +2.16 |
| Gold (n = 1,300/arm) | 19.38% | 21.92% | +2.54 |
| Platinum (n = 800/arm) | 35.63% | 35.13% | −0.50 |

All 10 parity checks pass (pandas == DuckDB, exact).

## Conclusion
The control redemption rate (12.8%) sits just above the 11.0% planning value used for the
power calculation. The pooled lift is +2.69 pp before any testing; the per-tier pattern
already suggests the effect is concentrated in the lower tiers.

## Limitations
Descriptive only — no test, no CI here (that is 03). The per-tier n varies 8× by design
(Gold/Platinum were oversampled, see docs/01 §7.1).

## Recommendation
Proceed to the randomisation check (02) before interpreting any of these differences.
