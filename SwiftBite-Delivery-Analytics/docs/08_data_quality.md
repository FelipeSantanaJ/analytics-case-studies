> deixar passar. Oito classes (Q01–Q08), cada uma com um contador no `dq_report.md` e,

---

## 1. Philosophy

Every messy trait is **injected on purpose** by `01_generate_raw.py`, **handled** by `02`
(parse / repair) or `03` (conform / sentinel), **counted** into a per-entity DQ fragment,
and — where it is an invariant — **asserted** in `dq_checks.py`, which fails the build.
Raw is never edited after generation. One `SEED` makes the whole thing reproducible, so the
counters below are stable.

## 3. Operational identities asserted (hard)

- **Order conservation**, every zone-hour: `orders_placed == orders_delivered +
  Σ cancelled_{customer, courier, no_courier, restaurant}` (exact).
- **Rate sanity:** `fulfillment_rate ∈ [0, 1]`; `eta_p50_min ≤ eta_p90_min`;
  `liquidity_ratio ≥ 0`; `idle_courier_ratio ∈ [0, 1]`.
- **Experiment integrity:** every `fact_experiment_zone_day` zone-day exists in
  `fact_incentive_assignment`; treated zone-days have `bonus_brl_per_delivery > 0`, control
  `== 0`; ≥ 75 days of post-experiment fact data.

## 4. Benchmark bands checked (soft — reported, not fatal)

On the **pre-experiment baseline** (window-months 1–15) the following must sit mid-band, or
the calibration is off (see `01_business_context_kpis.md` §9):

| Metric | Band | Current build |
|---|---|---|
| Fulfillment rate | 0.93 – 0.995 | **0.956** ✅ |
| ETA p50 (min) | 24 – 42 | **≈ 38** ✅ |
| ETA p90 (min) | 42 – 80 | **≈ 46** ✅ |
| Cancellation rate | 0.01 – 0.07 | **0.044** ✅ |
| Idle-courier ratio | 0.10 – 0.40 | **0.286** ✅ |
| Courier earnings / active-hour (BRL) | 20 – 40 | **≈ 33** ✅ |
| Per-stratum arm allocation | 0.45 – 0.55 | **0.50** all 12 ✅ |

The experiment arms are allowed to diverge inside the window — that is the point.
